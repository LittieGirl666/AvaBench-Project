# Compact paper results

The included files retain the numerical observations needed for the main results and Appendices B–C. Behavioral records come from the final three-model findings; mechanism records come from the full-cohort Stage 1 and Stage 2 experiments.

| File | Rows | Paper use |
|---|---:|---|
| `behavior/structural_cases.csv` | 8,400 | Figure 2; structural outcomes in Appendix B |
| `behavior/semantic_cases.csv` | 4,200 | Figure 3; semantic outcomes and remaining wrong state in Appendix B |
| `stage1/pair_effects.csv` | 2,843 | Tables 4–5 and all structural comparisons in Table 7 |
| `stage1/example_scores.json` | 8 contexts | Complete candidate sequence scores for one structural and one semantic task, both models and conditions |
| `stage2/paired_effects.csv` | 19,788 | Figure 4 and late-block effects in Table 6 |
| `stage2/control_effects.csv` | 4,288 | Same-condition donor comparisons in Table 6 |
| `tables/` | — | CSV summaries and LaTeX table bodies |

## Column meanings

Behavior rows identify the model, task, condition, termination, outcome, and interaction cost. `behavior_correct` means completion when reachable or correct unavailability when unreachable. Semantic rows also record decoy calls and `residual_wrong_state_at_termination`. Structural rows leave state-changing metrics empty.

Stage 1 rows contain `high_margin`, `low_margin`, and `effect`. H/L mean complete/missing for structure and success/failure for semantics. Consequently, `effect` is **L − H for structure** and **H − L for semantics**. `phenotype` associates structural replays with their natural-run stopping outcome; `cluster_id` identifies the operation or state-action group.

The candidate-score examples expose the inputs to the margin calculation. `sequence_logprob` is the sum of action-token log probabilities; `legal_in_context` identifies names visible in that replay context. Structural grouping uses the visible environment actions plus the two alternative controls.

Stage 2 rows contain clean and patched preferences, the clean gap, and the donor-oriented effect. `confirmation` rows are L35 measurements on the full selected cohort. `external_confirmation` rows are the layer sweep on the complementary subset. L35 appears in both summaries for that subset; choose the appropriate split before aggregation. Control rows identify the cross-task activation donor and retain the clean paired donor as the reference for effect direction.

To rebuild all summaries and plots:

```bash
python -m analysis.reproduce
```

The analysis recomputes descriptive means and counts from these rows. It preserves task-group weighting for interventions and pair weighting for Stage 1 scores. Full dialogues, model weights, and activation tensors are generated by the experiment commands rather than bundled with the recorded results.
